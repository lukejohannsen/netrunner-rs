# Netrunner Workspace Roadmap

**The single source of truth for status.** `AGENTS.md` is the rules of engagement, `ARCHITECTURE.md` the shape of the engine. This file is the index: where each area stands, what is next, what is known to be broken, and which area roadmap under `docs/roadmap/` holds the record. **Open an area roadmap only when working in that area.**

Code and docs address entries as `ROADMAP <phase> §<n>` — "Phase 2 §5 item 35", "Rules Audit T8", "Phase 1 §9 Stage 5", "RWR Stage 3b". Those addresses are permanent. An open or recently closed entry resolves in its area file; a closed one resolves **verbatim, under the same heading, in `docs/roadmap/archive/`** (`docs/roadmap/archive/README.md`), where the full record — decision, alternative rejected, numbers — is kept and never re-read to orient. The area file keeps one sentence per closed item.

**Current goal:** a solid single-player Netrunner, then network play. "Solid" means a person can feel it: the whole NetrunnerDB card pool playable in the DSL (Phase 1 §9), bots that play the way the strategy guide teaches, in a style a deck chooses (Phase 5 §25), and a difficulty ladder to climb (Phase 5, done). A *rating* is between people and a server's to keep (Phase 4 §5); a game against a bot is casual. The search research (Phase 2 §5) is parked behind all of that since 13 September 2026 — its history is in the archive's copy of this file.

**Health (29 September 2026, `main` at #294):** CI green — `cargo test --workspace` (about 2,300 test functions), `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` silent, both sweeps clean under the rules-coverage gate, the card-conservation invariant and the fog gate with no stall tolerance. `ActionSpace::SIZE` 3261, `OBS_SIZE` 6423, `CARD_VOCAB` 1024, `MAX_STEPS` 2,500, `DECISION_BUDGET` 256. The shipped bots carry no data: `Random`, `Heuristic` with its eight `Personality` profiles, `Mcts` and `Puct` are pure code; `puct-onnx` is behind an off-by-default feature and no trained network has beaten the search it would replace.

## How this file is kept

- Status lives here or in an area roadmap, never in `AGENTS.md`. A new entry goes in the area file under its numbered section; then update the table row and the list above.
- An entry records the decision, the alternative rejected, and the load-bearing numbers (before → after, seeds, game counts, branch). Narrative is compressed once the decision is taken; a number is never dropped.
- **When an item closes, its entry moves verbatim to `docs/roadmap/archive/<same file>.md` under the same heading, in the PR that closes it**, and the live file keeps one sentence and the address. The table row here is one sentence per area, and never longer than a few lines: the index is read every session.
- A later entry that corrects an earlier one says so in both places rather than deleting the earlier text.
- A bug found and not fixed goes on the list above with its owner; a bug fixed comes off it.
