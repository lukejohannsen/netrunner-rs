# Netrunner Workspace Roadmap

**The single source of truth for status.** `AGENTS.md` is the rules of engagement, `ARCHITECTURE.md` the shape of the engine. This file is the index: where each area stands, what is next, what is known to be broken, and which area roadmap under `docs/roadmap/` holds the record. **Open an area roadmap only when working in that area.**

Code and docs address entries as `ROADMAP <phase> §<n>` — "Phase 2 §5 item 35", "Rules Audit T8", "Phase 1 §9 Stage 5", "RWR Stage 3b". Those addresses are permanent. An open or recently closed entry resolves in its area file; a closed one resolves **verbatim, under the same heading, in `docs/roadmap/archive/`** (`docs/roadmap/archive/README.md`), where the full record — decision, alternative rejected, numbers — is kept and never re-read to orient. The area file keeps one sentence per closed item.

**Current goal:** a solid single-player Netrunner, then network play. "Solid" means a person can feel it: the whole NetrunnerDB card pool playable in the DSL (Phase 1 §9), bots that play the way the strategy guide teaches, in a style a deck chooses (Phase 5 §25), and a difficulty ladder to climb (Phase 5, done). A *rating* is between people and a server's to keep (Phase 4 §5); a game against a bot is casual. The search research (Phase 2 §5) is parked behind all of that since 13 September 2026 — its history is in the archive's copy of this file.

**Health (29 September 2026, `main` at #294):** CI green — `cargo test --workspace` (about 2,300 test functions), `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` silent, both sweeps clean under the rules-coverage gate, the card-conservation invariant and the fog gate with no stall tolerance. `ActionSpace::SIZE` 3261, `OBS_SIZE` 6423, `CARD_VOCAB` 1024, `MAX_STEPS` 2,500, `DECISION_BUDGET` 256. The shipped bots carry no data: `Random`, `Heuristic` with its eight `Personality` profiles, `Mcts` and `Puct` are pure code; `puct-onnx` is behind an off-by-default feature and no trained network has beaten the search it would replace.

## What is next, in order

1. **The whole NetrunnerDB pool** (Phase 1 §9; [nsg-card-pool.md](docs/roadmap/nsg-card-pool.md)): every card NetrunnerDB lists expressed in the DSL, set by set behind a completeness gate, one mechanic per stage, the rule read first and its conformance row updated, and each stage's UI drawn with it. **Parhelion Stage 3 is next** (advancement requirement as a `ContinuousKind`, and the Corp words). Then Midnight Sun, Uprising, Downfall, the reprints.
2. **Bots that play the strategy guide's precepts** (Phase 5 §25): the decision core rebuilt on the existing harness — a precepts report, what a bot knows (the format's pool, a faction-and-seen-copies prior over the opponent's deck, memory), the evaluator as plan modules with a board-read stage, a whole-turn planner, economy at the guide's rate, Corp plans a deck stacks and Runner plans from the faction, then both ladders re-taken on the new bot. Stage 0 (29 September 2026) made "Startup is complete" a gate and `--format` a filter on every measurement; Stage 1 (the same day) built the report and took the baseline; Stage 2 (the same day) gave every bot `Knowledge` — the format's pool, a weighted prior over the opponent's deck and a memory — and the report a guess-quality line; Stage 3 (the same day) split the evaluator into modules byte-identically, made the stage a reading in `eval::stage`, and deleted the stage dial; Stage 4 (the same day) built the turn planner beside the one-ply reference and measured it: the guide's from-hand and never-advance lines are played for the first time, twenty-one never-used cards are reached, and the Corp chair loses 0.11 to the reference because a click, a card and the opponent's credits are priced by no term yet; Stage 5 (the same day) priced them at the guide's rate, eight terms the planner alone scores with and the reference does not, measured one at a time on the bench legs: the Corp chair is level with the reference again and the Runner chair 0.08 past it, and a Corp hand priced held was the one term that lost (a planner holds what it values, it does not draw toward it); Stage 6 (the same day) replaced `Personality` with the plans a deck stacks (`plans::{Plan, Style}`, `DeckFile::style` a list, the reference playing the first plan's profile unmoved) and gave the planner the Corp's plans as terms — the stakes of a run and the rez it holds, the ICE order, the never-advance line's condition, the kill plan's lethal check and tag leverage with a Sweep deck that holds the payoffs, the traps plan's bluffed asset, and the fort falling away once the rig beats the wall when fast advance follows glacier. **Stage 7 is next.** Each stage is measured against the fixed one-ply reference on pinned binaries *and* by the precepts report. What is already known: a stage dial between profiles lost on both chairs; a *where*, read off the board (the fort terms), won.
3. **A person playing Phase 6 end to end**: the menu, the deck builder and online play across two machines, by someone playing a real game rather than a tmux drive.
4. **Online play between people** (Phase 4 §7 stages 4b–4d, 5, 6; §5 (e)): staying attached across games, lobby browsing in both clients, rating inside lobbies, tournaments (design recorded in §7 stage 6), and the free take-back online.
5. **The desktop client's owed feel** (Phase 7 §4, §8, §9): movement and sound, replay notes, and decklists searched and downloaded from NetrunnerDB.
6. **Rules work** (Rules Audit items 9 and 10; Conformance B4, E3, F6 and the not-modelled rows): each taken when a pool card needs it, except the scenario builder, which is test infrastructure.

**Parked** — the search research (Phase 2 §5), kept where it stood: the Runner chair's next lever is the playout policy (`mcts::action_weight` plays both chairs through every playout) rather than a bigger search (items 38–43); `mcts` chair numbers were taken at `DEFAULT_TREES = 4` (item 41); deeper batching waits for a run that wants compute (item 27); whether the pool is Corp-favoured at all is a re-measurement once the Runner chair is fixed (item 34, `scripts/corp_share_curve.py`).

## How this file is kept

- Status lives here or in an area roadmap, never in `AGENTS.md`. A new entry goes in the area file under its numbered section; then update the table row and the list above.
- An entry records the decision, the alternative rejected, and the load-bearing numbers (before → after, seeds, game counts, branch). Narrative is compressed once the decision is taken; a number is never dropped.
- **When an item closes, its entry moves verbatim to `docs/roadmap/archive/<same file>.md` under the same heading, in the PR that closes it**, and the live file keeps one sentence and the address. The table row here is one sentence per area, and never longer than a few lines: the index is read every session.
- A later entry that corrects an earlier one says so in both places rather than deleting the earlier text.
- A bug found and not fixed goes on the list above with its owner; a bug fixed comes off it.
