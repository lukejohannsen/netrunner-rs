# Netrunner Workspace Roadmap

**The single source of truth for status.** `AGENTS.md` is the rules of engagement, `ARCHITECTURE.md` the shape of the engine. This file is the index: where each area stands, what is next, and which area roadmap under `docs/roadmap/` holds the record. **Open an area roadmap only when working in that area** — each is the full log of decisions and measurements for its phase, and none is needed to orient.

Code and docs address entries as `ROADMAP <phase> §<n>` — "Phase 2 §5", "Rules Audit T8", "Phase 1 §8 Stage 5", "Phase 1.75 §6". Those addresses are permanent and resolve through the table below; an area roadmap keeps its section numbers even when an item is compressed to a line.

**Current goal:** a solid single-player Netrunner, then network play.

**Health (10 September 2026):** `cargo test --workspace` green (about 1,200 tests), `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` silent, both 256-seed sweeps clean under the rules-coverage gate, the card-conservation invariant and the fog gate, with **no stall tolerance** — every seating reaches `GameOver`. `ActionSpace::SIZE` 1646, `OBS_SIZE` 2262, `CARD_VOCAB` 192, `MAX_STEPS` 2,500, `DECISION_BUDGET` 256. **The shipped bots are complete and carry no data**: `Random`, `Heuristic` with its six `Personality` profiles, `Mcts` and `Puct` are pure code — `eval::Weights` constants compiled into `netrunner_bots`, no file read at run time. The one bot that consumes a file, `puct-onnx`, sits behind an off-by-default feature, and **no trained network has yet beaten the `Puct` search it would replace** as a whole: four volume runs, none promoted. **Its two halves have now come apart** (§5 item 22, 8 September 2026) — a checkpoint trained on this engine scores **0.617 priors-only**, over the 0.55 threshold and above baseline on both chairs, against **0.141 value-only**, and seating both gives 0.359. **The fifth run then seated the priors alone and found they do not compound** (§5 item 26): over 1,152 arena games the candidate is −0.8σ from parity. **The "seat trade" read off that run's chair split is withdrawn** (§5 item 32, 10 September 2026): a chair's null is the pool's Corp win rate at that configuration — measured **0.6823 / 0.3177** — not 0.500, so the ±0.22 chair deltas are +0.026 and −0.050 and the candidate is at parity on both chairs. The per-chair promotion floor built on that reading (§5 item 28) was centred on 0.500 and unreachable, and **§5 item 33 re-centres it on a null leg measured per incumbent per arena shape** (`--arena-null`), which restores the gate's ability to promote without changing a single recorded verdict; the `(chair, outcome)` sample weight (§5 item 29) is a small real gain, cb05 being the one leg at or above its null on both chairs. The retired runs and their checkpoints are under `data/runs/`; the corpora are deleted.

## What is next, in order

1. **Why does seating a policy head move the pool ten points toward the Corp?** (Phase 2 §5 items 31, 32.) This is the one thing that survived the chair diagnostic and it is now a cleaner question than it was. It is not a per-seat defect in the head — the head is equally faithful to the search on both chairs by every offline measure — and it moves *both* players together: uniform search is 54.8% Corp in self-play and 0.635 greedy, priors-only is 64.9% in self-play and 0.682 / 0.714 greedy. So the shape of it is "this deck pool favours the Corp as play sharpens", which is a game-balance question and is measurable where game balance already is: `bench`, the heuristic ladder, and the `--all-matchups` random-vs-random reports, rather than inside another training loop. Whether it needs fixing at all is part of the question — a Corp-favoured pool is a fact about the decks, not a bug in the trainer, and the only thing it certainly breaks is reading chair scores without a null.

2. **Deeper batching, if a run ever wants it** (Phase 2 §5 item 27). Cross-game batching landed at 2.6× with a byte-identical corpus, and the measured ceiling is higher still — 4 runners at batch 32 reach 46,229 rows/s against the ~11,300 the loop now achieves — but reaching it needs more rows outstanding than ~64 game threads produce. Leaf parallelization inside a search (virtual loss) would supply them and **does** change search results, so it needs the full strength bar. Not worth it until a run is actually waiting on compute.

3. **The PUCT Runner chair — both leads closed, and the seating's noise floor established** (Phase 3 §1, last bullet). Measured over seeds 1, 2 and 3 rather than seed 1 alone, which changed the answer. **The identity-carry lead dissolved**: the effect changes sign in two of three configurations and its mean never leaves the band (−0.016 / −0.002 / −0.016 against a band of 0.026–0.047), so the earlier "negative every time it was tried" is withdrawn as a one-seed reading and there is nothing left to explain. **The chance node is real** (−0.038, sd 0.008, same sign on three seeds) **and the budget is not its reason** — four outcomes at 512 simulations, giving the children what one sample had at 128, recovers nothing. Underneath both: **this search is not budget-bound**, 128 → 512 simulations being worth 0.483 → 0.481. The next lead here must clear a **seed-spread band of 0.026–0.047 over 192 games** and must not be a change that only spends more budget.
4. Small open items, each recorded in its area file: `netrunner_gym` can still toggle-loop; the coverage card gate is inert at default seeds for decks the sweep has not played eight times; `netrunner_single_player/tests/common/mod.rs` still carries a filler fixture; Core Set cards have no sweep coverage.

## How this file is kept

- Status lives here or in an area roadmap, never in `AGENTS.md`. A new entry goes in the area file under its numbered section; then update the table row and the "next" list here.
- An entry records the decision, the alternative rejected, and the load-bearing numbers (before → after, seeds, game counts, branch). Narrative is compressed at the next pass; numbers and decisions are not.
- A later entry that corrects an earlier one says so in both places rather than deleting the earlier text.
